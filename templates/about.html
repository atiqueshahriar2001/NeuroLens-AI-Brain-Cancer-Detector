NOTEBOOK METHODS

How to interpret the analysis

Explainability

Grad-CAM++ and Integrated Gradients show model attribution maps. EAS compares the top activation regions between methods.

Uncertainty

MC Dropout reports mean probabilities, per-class spread and predictive entropy. Notebook reliability bands use validation entropy quartiles.

Robustness

The notebook evaluates noise, blur, brightness, contrast and rotation over a test image set. This app does not claim those aggregate scores for a single uploaded image.

The notebook also describes dataset auditing, baseline evaluation, external validation, resource profiling and ablation. Those are offline experiments and are not recomputed by this web interface.

Research and education only
Experimental output only. Do not use this result as a diagnosis or as a replacement for qualified medical assessment.
